What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a way to invest a fixed amount at regular intervals, commonly monthly, into a mutual fund or another investment product that supports recurring contributions. The amount invested and the investment value can change over time.

How SIP returns are estimated

This calculator uses a monthly compounding model. For a monthly investment P, monthly rate r and n monthly contributions, the estimated future value is P × ((1+r)n − 1) ÷ r × (1+r). When the expected return is zero, the future value equals the total amount invested.

How to use the SIP calculator

  1. Enter the amount you plan to invest every month.
  2. Enter an assumed annual return.
  3. Choose the investment period.
  4. Review total investment, estimated returns and estimated maturity value.

Example

If you invest ₹10,000 per month for 10 years and assume a 12% annual return, the calculator estimates the potential future value using monthly compounding. The actual result may be different because investment returns are not fixed.

Frequently asked questions

Is SIP return guaranteed?

No. A SIP is an investment method, not a guaranteed-return product. The result shown here is an estimate based on the return assumption you enter.

Can I change the monthly SIP amount?

Yes. Enter any positive monthly contribution supported by your investment plan and compare different scenarios.

Does this calculator require my personal information?

No. It only uses the numbers you enter in the browser to perform the calculation.
