What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the regular amount paid toward a loan each month. An EMI generally contains both interest and a portion of the principal, with the interest component usually higher at the beginning of a standard reducing-balance loan.

EMI formula

For a reducing-balance loan, EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments. For a zero-interest loan, EMI is simply the principal divided by the number of payments.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this for a home loan?

Yes. Enter the home loan principal, annual interest rate and tenure to estimate the monthly payment.

Can I use this for a personal loan?

Yes. The same reducing-balance EMI formula can be used for many personal, auto and home loans. Check your lender's repayment method for the exact amount.

Does the calculator store my loan details?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and the values are not uploaded by this page.
