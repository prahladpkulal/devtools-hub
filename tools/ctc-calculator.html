CTC / Monthly Salary Calculator

Convert annual CTC to a simple monthly CTC estimate.

This is an estimate, not a tax or payroll calculation.

How CTC is converted to monthly salary

Annual CTC divided by 12 gives a simple monthly CTC estimate. If annual bonus is supplied, the calculator also shows the monthly fixed component before deductions.

Actual take-home salary depends on tax, PF, employer contributions, bonus structure and other payroll components.
