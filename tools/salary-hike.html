Salary Hike Calculator

Enter your old and new annual CTC to calculate the raise.

How salary hike is calculated

The percentage hike is calculated as (new CTC − old CTC) ÷ old CTC × 100. The calculator also shows the annual and monthly CTC difference.

Actual take-home pay depends on salary structure, income tax, PF, bonus and other components.
