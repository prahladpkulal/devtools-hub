What DevTools Hub does

DevTools Hub provides free browser-based developer, finance and professional utilities. Where practical, calculations and developer-tool processing happen in your browser. Input is not intentionally uploaded to a DevTools Hub server just to perform these local calculations.

Information collected

The site does not require an account or ask you to submit personal information to use its tools. Standard web requests may include technical information such as your IP address, browser type and requested URL as part of normal internet delivery and security.

Analytics

Analytics may be enabled in the future to understand aggregate traffic, popular tools and site performance. If enabled, the relevant analytics provider may collect information such as page views, approximate location, device and browser information according to its own privacy policy.

Advertising

DevTools Hub may display advertising in the future. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies where permitted to measure advertising performance or personalize ads. Any required consent or privacy controls will be added before such services are enabled.

Cookies and local storage

Individual tools may use browser capabilities such as local storage only when needed for functionality. Third-party services, when enabled, may set their own cookies or similar identifiers.

Third-party services

External services can have their own privacy policies and terms. DevTools Hub does not control how third-party providers process information once their services are enabled.

Children

DevTools Hub is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's features, analytics or advertising services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact
